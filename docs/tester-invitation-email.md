**Please follow the process in this order**

**1. Sign in as the Examiner and start an assessment (Phase 1)**
- Open **My subjects** and check that PHE261S is ticked (the practice examiner already has it).
- Click **New assessment**. Choose PHE261S, enter "Test 1", pick a date about 30 days from today (the system works out the deadlines: pre-moderation 14 days before the assessment, post-moderation 14 days after), choose the internal moderator, and add the external moderator.
- Complete **Section 1**: period, year, HEQF level, qualification and the question-type weightings (they must add up to 100%).
- Upload a paper and a memorandum. You can use the sample files in the project's `docs/samples` folder, or any Word (`.docx`) or PDF file. A password-protected Word file also works; tell your moderator the password.
- Draw your signature, enter your password and click **Sign & submit**.

**2. Sign in as the Internal moderator: Gate 1, pre-moderation**
- Open the record marked **Your turn**.
- Read the paper in the browser. For Word files, select any text to add a comment; the comment is saved at once.
- Give each criterion a rating of 0, 1 or 2, and answer the three Section 1 questions.
- First try **Request revision** (the examiner receives your comments). Then, as the Examiner, mark the comments as addressed, upload a corrected paper and re-submit.
- Back as the moderator, tick **Consensus reached**, sign and click **Sign & approve**.

**3. Examiner: post-assessment (Section 2)**
- Upload the class list or marks workbook (an `.xls`, `.xlsx` or `.csv`). The sample is in `docs/samples`. The system calculates pass rate, average and distribution automatically.
- Enter the number of registered students, the type of assessment and answer Section 2 questions 1–5.
- Sign and submit for final moderation.

**4. Internal moderator: Gate 2, final review**
- Check the statistics, answer questions 6 and 7, comment on the items, state whether any mark adjustment is recommended, then sign and approve.

**5. External moderator: Gate 3, Section 3**
- The record only appears for the external moderator after Gate 2. Complete the Section 3 comments and adjustments and sign.

**6. Examiner and Head of Department: sign Section 3**
- Sign in as the Examiner and sign Section 3, then as the Head of Department and sign. The last signature completes the record.

**7. Download the report**
- Open the completed record and download the PDF. Please compare it with the official form.
